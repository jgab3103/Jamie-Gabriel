Waltz for Agnes Transcription (Marek Dykta)

Yeah so I totally thought this was written by John Abercrombie who plays on this duo album. I was so wrong! But, wow, this transcription reveals some wonderful harmonic moments: the movement from E♭m7(sus4) to G7(#5), that ambiguity of A/B, and later the luminous E♭maj7(#5). Boom! So good.
