A small listening experiment

Begin with only stepwise movement. Then raise the thirds and listen for a more arpeggiated shape. Add fourths and notice the openness they introduce. Finally, change modes while the C drone continues: the root stays fixed, but the sense of colour shifts around it.

There is no score to follow and no correct answer. This is an exercise in hearing the character of a scale through repeated, evolving musical gestures.
