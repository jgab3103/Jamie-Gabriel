Reading the timeline

Each grey bar represents a composer's lifespan, from birth to death. The coloured section indicates their approximate period of musical activity. These dates are intended as broad historical guides rather than definitive boundaries of creative work.

Hover over a composer to see their dates. Click a composer to highlight other composers whose active years overlapped. Click the same composer again to clear the selection.

Musical period classifications are necessarily approximate. Composers whose careers span different stylistic eras have been assigned a single broad category for this visualisation.
