Music Lessons in Fennell Bay

September 2026

We've just moved into our new place in Fennell Bay, and I'm really looking forward to teaching and playing music here. There is still plenty of renovating to do, a studio to set up, and what feels like an endless number of boxes to unpack, but we're getting there! It will be great to have both a dedicated music studio and seperate music room for recitals and playing. We have so many scores and music resources we have collected over the years (and when I say we, I mean by far it has been Sally developing these!) Finally, there is a library to squeeze them into!

I am also excited that that we have just sourced a beautiful piano, which is arriving in mid-October 2026. It will be wonderful to have an acoustic instrument in the house for playing, practising and, of course, teaching all the nuances of performance on pianos.

Lessons and availability

It is has been a crazy year of transition to new places, but looks we will be available to teach piano, guitar and all things music, and love working with students of different ages, I have a little availability for private lessons during Term 4, 2026, so please feel free to get in touch if you're interested in organising lessons.

National Music Academy

I have also taken on a little teaching at the National Music Academy during Term 4, at their Maitland and Newcastle West locations. It's great to be working with these students across the region and fun to be getting back into regular music teaching.
