About

I started out a little undecided as to whether to pursue music or computer science. I ended up going with music first, studying first at Australian Institute of Music and the Sydney Conservatorium of Music, before completing postgraduate studies at Macquarie University and the University of Technology Sydney.

I hold a PhD in computational musicology focused on machine learning approaches to music analysis.

I spent a number of years teaching in tertiary settings, on subjects like musicology, harmony, improvisation, music history, ensemble practice, and composition. I also became increasingly involved in computer science and math and over the past two decades I have developed all kinds of software in languages like C, C++, Java, R, Haskell, and Python, which included a large scale digital humanities project for the search and anlaysis of large XML orchestral datasets.

In 2012 I made the move out of academia into more pure data science and machine learning roles. I worked primarily in health analytics and developed deep learning systems and data security solutions across a range of corporate and government roles.

This culminated in my appointment as Chief Data Officer at the Cancer Institute NSW, a role I held from 2018 until 2025. It was a pretty amazing time, giving me the opportunity to lead data and analytics strategy at scale, manage large data science and technology teams, contribute to data and analytics architecture in the health space, and participate in AI strategy at the state and federal level.

In 2025, I decided to step back from data and technology to find time for music and teaching again. I started working with a number of different ensembles, and started carving out time for composition once again. It has been a crazy ride. I also returned to teaching, but this time in schools focusing on both primary and secondary education.

As a performing musician, I am currently working across solo and ensemble contexts that span jazz fusion, experimental improvisation, and electronic ambient music. When playing live, I like to mix it all up, acoustic and soundscape, looping and samples and a committment to an improvisation-first approach.

I have also recently released a first album, SAJA Project 01 with Aotearoa based artist Sally Jamila. It has been amazing to make this recording with my partner in music and in life, and we are looking forward to recording more music soon.

I think that is about it. I have two beautiful children and try and get to the water every day. Sometimes I think I should get a dog and do (at least some) yoga.

Having moved between two careers, I have found it is simpler to have two different CV's. One outlines my experience in software development, enterprise technologies, data science and executive roles.

The other is focused on my music, teaching and research.
