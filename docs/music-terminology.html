About musical terminology

Italian has traditionally been the dominant language of musical notation, particularly for tempo, dynamics and articulation. However, composers have also used French, German and other languages to communicate musical intentions.

Not every musical term has an exact equivalent in another language. Some translations are descriptive, while others reflect conventions used by composers in different historical and cultural contexts.
