SAJA Music

In 2025, I co-founded SAJA Music with Sally Howe, a non-profit music education platform dedicated to making high-quality learning resources freely accessible to everyone.

After a crazy year of pulling it together, SAJA MUSIC entered beta on 3 November 2026, and we're really excited to share what we've been working on! Our collection of lessons, interactive exercises and teaching resources continues to grow, with plenty more to come.

You can discover the learning resources we've created, or follow our learning pathways to explore music at your own pace.

We're also still finding time to teach from our studio in Fennell Bay, Lake Macquarie. If you're interested in private music lessons, just get in touch. Get in touch through SAJA Music.
