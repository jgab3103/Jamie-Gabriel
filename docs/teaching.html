Teaching

I have extensive experience teaching and mentoring people across a wide range of ages, backgrounds and levels of experience — from young beginners and school students to university and postgraduate musicians. I have taught at university in Music, Music History, Computer Science and Mathematics, and throughout my professional career I have also mentored programmers, data scientists and other professionals. I have presented and taught at conferences, drawing on my background in music, computing, mathematics and academic research.

I am qualified to teach in both Primary and Secondary settings and am currently completing a Master of Teaching (Secondary) with teaching areas in Music and History. Alongside teaching, I work as a musician, composer and researcher, bringing together practical musical experience with a strong academic and technical background. This allows me to move comfortably between traditional musicianship, creative practice and contemporary music technology.

I am particularly passionate about helping students discover their own way into music. I have experience working with neurodiverse students and value flexible, individualised approaches to learning that recognise different strengths, interests and ways of thinking. Whether developing fundamental musical skills, exploring improvisation and composition, preparing for formal study, or experimenting with technology, I aim to create an encouraging environment where students can build confidence while developing genuine musical independence.
